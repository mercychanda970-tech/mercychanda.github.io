About Me

Curious learner,
purposeful innovator.

I am a final-year Cyber Security student at Kapasa Makasa University, committed to using technology as a force for good — building solutions that are accessible, locally relevant, and truly impactful for Zambian communities.

My Story

Growing up in Zambia gave me a deep appreciation for the transformative power of technology — and an equally clear view of the barriers that prevent most people from benefiting from it. That contrast drives everything I do as a developer and as a person.

At Kapasa Makasa University, I have developed a strong foundation in software engineering, database management, and human-computer interaction. My projects consistently focus on solving real problems for real people — especially those in underserved rural and peri-urban communities.

As a fourth-year student, I have had the privilege of leading team projects, mentoring junior peers, and participating in national hackathons. I believe that the best technology is built by diverse teams who understand the lived experiences of the people they design for.

Beyond academics, I am an active advocate for women in STEM, a peer tutor, and a volunteer with community digital-literacy initiatives. After graduating, I plan to work on fintech and agri-tech solutions that address Zambia's most pressing development challenges.

Inclusion & Diversity

I believe technology built by diverse teams, for diverse people, creates the most lasting impact. Here is how I actively champion inclusion:

👩‍💻

Actively recruit and mentor young women into Computer Science, co-organising campus coding workshops that have engaged 150+ female students.

♿

Accessible Design

All my projects follow WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines — readable fonts, colour contrast, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader compatibility.

🌍

Building software with support for Bemba and other Zambian languages so technology is truly accessible to all citizens, not just English speakers.

Running monthly peer-study groups for first-generation university students navigating the challenges of Computer Science for the first time.
